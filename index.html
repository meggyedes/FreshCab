About FreshCab

Welcome to FreshCab, your trusted mobile car detailing service in the Netherlands.

I'm Daniel, the owner and operator of FreshCab. I worked as a detailer for 1.5 years in Hungary before moving to the Netherlands. I specialize in delivering quality work and great attention to detail. As a car enthusiast, I understand that your car is more than just transportation — it's your personal space, and it deserves to be clean, fresh, and comfortable.

Since I wasn’t able to bring all my professional equipment with me when relocating, at the moment I offer a selection of essential detailing services. However, my goal is to continuously expand and upgrade my tools here in the Netherlands so that I can soon provide a wider range of advanced, high-quality services.

As a mobile service, I bring eco-friendly cleaning products and the necessary equipment directly to your location — whether you're at home, at work, or anywhere within my service area. Convenience and care, delivered to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most interior cleaning services take between 1-2 hours, depending on the package you choose and the condition of your vehicle. Basic packages are typically faster, while premium services with deep cleaning take longer, up to 4 hours.

You don't need to be present for the entire service, but I'll need access to your vehicle at the start and to hand over the keys when finished. Many clients drop off their keys and continue with their day at home or work.

Since I wasn’t able to bring all of my professional equipment when relocating to the Netherlands, I currently work with essential, quality tools and cleaning products that are safe for all surfaces. My setup includes a powerful portable vacuum, interior brushes, microfiber cloths, and effective cleaning solutions. As I continue to expand my equipment here, I’ll be able to offer even more advanced services over time.

I accept cash, bank transfer, and mobile payment apps. Payment is due upon completion of the service. I'll provide you with all payment details when we schedule your appointment.

I come to you! That's the beauty of mobile detailing. I'll bring all necessary equipment and supplies to your home, office, or any location within the service area. All I need is access to your car.

No special preparation is required. If you can, please remove any personal belongings beforehand to help ensure thorough cleaning. If not, I’ll safely set them aside during the service and return them to the car in a clean plastic bag once I’m finished.

I can improve most stains and odors with my current tools and products. Some older or deeper stains may not disappear completely, but I always work to achieve the best possible result safely. As I expand my equipment, I’ll be able to offer deeper restoration options.

Light weather conditions are fine, but heavy rain or strong wind can affect the cleaning process. If the weather is unsuitable, we can reschedule at no extra cost to ensure the best results.
